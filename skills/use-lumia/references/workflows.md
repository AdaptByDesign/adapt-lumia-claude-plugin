# Lumia workflow examples

These examples describe requests and useful outcomes. Follow the connected MCP
tools for execution instructions and availability.

| Example request | Useful outcome |
| --- | --- |
| "Which organisations am I connected to?" | A readable organisation list with the relevant connection context. |
| "Who was my latest Career Valuation with?" | The matching record's date, participants and relevant details. |
| "Summarise our objectives this quarter." | A progress summary highlighting achievements, outstanding work and gaps in the available evidence. |
| "How does Career Valuation work?" | A concise explanation supported by relevant Lumia help articles. |
| "Who can edit people's positions in our organisation?" | An explanation of the permitted actions and their scope, supported by available permission evidence. |
| "Show monthly active users for these organisations over the last quarter." | A comparison by organisation and month, with the measure and coverage explained. |
| "What are these organisations' subscription costs, and who are their leaders?" | A concise organisation comparison that identifies unavailable information. |
| "Which teams sit under this Key Function?" | A readable hierarchy answering the requested relationship. |
| "How have our Career Valuation results changed over the last year?" | A trend summary with its population, period and evidence limitations stated. |
| "Explain our latest team assessment results." | A summary connecting the available results to the meaning of the assessment questions. |
| "Which Preset Prompts can I use?" | An overview of the available analyses and their purpose. |
| "Run the culture sentiment Preset Prompt." | The selected analysis through the MCP-provided confirmation and preparation workflow. |
